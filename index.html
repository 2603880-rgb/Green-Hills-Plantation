About Our Plantation

Green Hills Coffee Plantation is a family-owned business dedicated to producing the highest quality coffee beans in the region. Our plantation is nestled in the rolling hills of the countryside, providing the perfect environment for our coffee plants to thrive.

Plantation Activities
Planting
The process of planting coffee seedlings in the plantation.
Watering
The process of providing water to the coffee plants.
Fertilizing
The process of applying fertilizers to the coffee plants.
Pruning
The process of trimming the coffee plants to promote growth.
Maintaining
The process of taking care of the coffee plants on a regular basis.
Harvesting
The process of picking the ripe coffee cherries from the plants.
Processing
The process of removing the coffee beans from the cherries and preparing them for roasting.
Roasting
The process of heating the green coffee beans to develop their flavor.
Packaging
The process of packing the roasted coffee beans for distribution and sale.
